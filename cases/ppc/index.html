Too many smells on one page.
01 · The problem

The page asked a paid visitor to do too much, too early.

Paid prospects weren't getting past the top of the page. Only 30% scrolled beyond the hero, and the page's exit rate was 60%: most people who clicked the ad left without seeing the product or the proof. The four leaks behind that are pinned on the old screenshot.

1Split hero: headline, body, email field, trial button and a photo collage compete for the first look.
2Messaging mismatch: the ad promised one thing, and the landing page talked about the whole platform.
3Proof (G2, SOC 2, logos) sat below the fold on most laptop screens.
4The product was barely explained: a visitor couldn't tell what they would get before being asked to sign up.
Where the budget goes

Five pages take most of the paid budget: business phone system, its demo page, auto-dialer, AI receptionist and SMS. Together they drew 19,828 paid sessions from May to October 2026, so those are the pages the revamp went after first.

02 · Hypothesis

If paid visitors see one offer, proof right under the CTA, and the product sorted by job instead of a crowded split hero, then trial starts will rise, because a paid visitor arrives with one intent from the ad: a page that matches it and offers one choice makes acting easier.

Primary: trial starts / visitorSecondary: engagement rate, scroll to pricingGuardrail: lead quality (MQL rate)
03 · What changed

Three design moves.

Move A · Hero
One column, one action

Headline names the job ("CRM-friendly business phone system"), three proof bullets, email + "Start Free Trial" in one row, risk-reversal microcopy underneath.

Live: justcall.io/business-phone-system ↗
Move B · Bento for the main offering
The product sorted by job, not by feature list

Six tabs, each one job with a real UI shot and one sentence of outcome ("Work the list without manual dialing"). Visitors self-select instead of scrolling past everything.

Live: the bento ↗
Move C · On-scroll widget
A floating section menu that follows the reader
Platform · Integrations · Customer stories · Security & compliance · Compare · Pricing · ROI calculator · Get started. It appears after the hero, highlights the current section, and keeps "Get started" one tap away.
Live: justcall.io ↗
Rolled out
The same three moves on other paid pages
Auto-dialer and CTI landing pages now use the one-column hero, proof under the CTA and job-sorted tabs.
Live: auto-dialer ↗

06 · What I chose not to test

Micro tests such as CTA colour, or "Book a demo" vs "Talk to sales" on the button. Paid traffic is expensive and limited, and small tweaks need long runtimes to show a small effect. I spent that traffic on structural changes that could move the number.

07 · What I'd test next

One page per campaign, retested as budgets change. Lower spend means longer runtimes, so fewer, bigger tests on the five pages above.

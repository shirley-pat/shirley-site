Case 04 · JustCall · Pricing page · 2024 onwards

About to leave pricing? A smaller ask. 340+ demos followed.

01 · The problem

The highest-intent page, and people left it with nothing.

Someone on the pricing page has already done the evaluating. When they leave without picking a plan, they leave as untagged lost traffic. The only ask on the page was the biggest one: commit to a plan.

02 · Hypothesis

If a pricing visitor heading for the tab sees a low-commitment offer, "book a demo" instead of "pick a plan", some will take it, because talking to someone first is a smaller step than buying.

Primary: demo form fills from the popup
03 · Result

340+ demo leads since 2024, all from pricing visitors who were on their way out and would otherwise have left with nothing.

04 · A pattern I'd used before
Kissflow's Digital Workplace Day: nearly 25% of people who saw the exit popup registered.

Together with a sticky footer CTA (+32% CTA clicks), registrations rose 28%. Same idea, different page: catch the leaver with a smaller, matching ask.

05 · What the demo flow taught me about asks

Smaller, clearer asks win. Extra steps lose.

06 · What I chose not to do

A discount in the popup. It would have pulled more form fills, but it trains buyers to wait for a deal and brings in leads who came for the price, not the product.

07 · What I'd test next

The same smaller ask on comparison and alternative pages, where visitors are also late in their evaluation, with the copy matched to the competitor they came to compare.

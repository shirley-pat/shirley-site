Case 03 · JustCall · Mobile demo flow · 2026

Six fields at once. One question per screen lifted demo requests 37%.

My role
CRO & Growth Marketing Manager: ran the test, directed the design
Team
1 designer (my team), a developer
Test
Launched 26 Aug 2026 · held 5 weeks
Mobile demo-page conversion11.1% → 15.2%
One sniff at a time.
01 · The problem

A desktop form, squeezed onto a phone.

The mobile demo page asked for everything up front: six fields on one screen, small touch targets, no sense of how much was left. Session recordings showed the pattern again and again: people opened the page, scrolled the form, saw how much it wanted, and left without filling in a single field.

02 · Hypothesis

If we redesign mobile demo booking with one question per screen, larger touch targets and fewer required fields, then demo conversion will rise, because lower effort at each step matches how people use a phone.

Primary: demo requests per visitorSecondary: demo-page conversion rateGuardrail: share of demos sent to sales
03 · What changed

Three design moves.

Step one is a tap, not typing
Team size as cards gets people moving before they reach a text field.
Show how little is left
A progress bar and short, friendly prompts. Bigger touch targets, fewer required fields.
End on the payoff
The flow ends on a real calendar with a named specialist, so the reward is immediate.
04 · Result
Demo requests per visitor+37%
Confidencep ≈ 0.01

Lead quality held

Source: Amplitude and GA4, JustCall. 25-week baseline vs 26 Aug – 27 Sep 2026. Traffic to the page was flat, so the lift came from the page.

05 · The part most people miss
The rate started to "fade". It hadn't.

A separate change later sent low-intent blog traffic to the demo page, and the conversion rate dipped. Reading demo requests per visitor instead showed the flow still worked: the new visitors were diluting the rate. Nobody rolled it back.

07 · What I chose not to test

Removing the phone field. It would have cut friction, but sales calls every demo lead first, so a lead without a number is a lead they can't work.

08 · What I'd test next
  • FOMO on the calendar step: show how few slots are left this week.
  • Step order: ask for the phone number before the email, since sales calls first.
  • Fill in the company name from the work email domain.
  • The same one-question-per-screen flow on desktop.

Stop 03 / About
Paid in treats.
the first agent on the team
Bruno here. Let me tell you about my human.

She keeps asking: did it work?

She started as a designer. At Ad2pro she made print and digital creative for publishers in the US, UK and Australia. She loved the craft. But she kept asking a question nobody around her was asking: did it work? I ask the same thing about every treat.

At Kissflow that question became her job. She brought UX research into marketing and ran 30+ internal sessions to get the team testing. Website engagement rose 40%. Blog-to-lead conversion rose 150%.

At JustCall she built the experimentation programme from scratch. 350+ tests later, across Kissflow and JustCall, she leads the design team and owns conversion across the site.

She still thinks like a designer. She just doesn't trust her taste until the data agrees. I don't trust anything until I've sniffed it. Same idea.

Her line, not mine

Conversion isn't a trick. It's clarity at the right moment.

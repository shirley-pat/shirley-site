Chapter 01 / The diagnosis

First, read the traffic that pays. Then find where it stops.

Comments
  • Every baseline and test is read on ICP traffic, where visitors convert almost twice as well.
  • Non-ICP markets send about half of all visitors but convert at roughly half the rate, so a blended number moves with traffic mix, not with the page.
  • Two non-ICP markets produced about half of non-ICP demo-page visits and almost no leads. That was bot traffic, and it is cleaned out before any read.
  • Against published benchmarks, visitor-to-demo sits at 2.59% versus a 1.5–2.5% median, counting only assigned demos, not requests.

Source: JustCall GA4 funnel dashboard and HubSpot, 1 May – 31 Oct 2026. Benchmark: Naoma demo benchmarks, 2026.

Chapter 02 / The strategy

Three priorities run the programme: capture demand, convert it, and measure it honestly.

Capture

Paid and brand pages

Match each ad to a page built for one action.

Goal: more leads at a lower cost per lead.

Convert

Homepage, pricing, demo

Find the step where visitors stop and test the change behind it.

Goal: lift visitor-to-lead.

Measure

ICP-only funnel

Read every baseline and test on the markets that pay.

Goal: results that hold up.

AI-native

Agents and skills I built handle competitor research, backlogs, site audits, tickets and reporting, so hypotheses arrive faster and analysis gets a second reading.

Every test starts from a written hypothesis that ties an observed friction to a proposed mechanism.

Chapter 05 / Convert

The homepage hero moved from describing the product to naming the buyer's problem.

Comments
  • Each refresh pushed the hero the same way: from "AI-Powered Customer Communication Platform" to "Stop Losing Customers to Faster Competitors".
  • The problem-led message was tested in paid search first, then moved to the homepage. The final wording came from a messaging grid built on 50+ customer interviews.
  • It is still the biggest leak: 86.5% of visits see only this page, so the next iteration changes positioning, not wording.

Source: justcall.io; JustCall GA4, ICP markets, May–Oct 2026.

One question per screen lifted mobile demo-page conversion by 37%.

Comments
  • Hypothesis: a single long form overloads mobile visitors. One question per screen, larger touch targets and fewer required fields reduce the effort.
  • Traffic to the page was flat, so the lift came from the page. Demo requests per visitor rose 37% and held for five weeks (p ≈ 0.01).
  • A separate change later sent low-intent blog traffic to the page and made the rate look like it was fading. Reading requests per visitor showed it was dilution.

Source: Amplitude and GA4, JustCall. Launched 26 Aug 2026; data through 27 Sep 2026.

One long form became a guided flow that asks one thing at a time.

Source: JustCall mobile demo flow, August 2026.

Vertical landing pages rebuilt with Product Marketing converted 45% better.

Comments
  • Pages were rewritten for one audience and one industry at a time, so the message matches the visitor.
  • I build and iterate pages myself in Figma, HTML and CSS, which takes a hypothesis to a live test without waiting on developers.
  • Interactive product demos built alongside them bring in 500+ qualified leads a month.

Source: GA4 and HubSpot, JustCall. Self-reported.

Chapter 06 / Where the next leaks are

33,277 sessions viewed pricing. Three in four left without a second page.

What the data shows
  • Team and Pro carry 81% of plan clicks. Business is 0.66%. Visitors cannot see who each plan is for.
  • US is 68% of pricing traffic. UK plan-click rate is 22.7%, nearly double the US at 12.0%.
  • Of the 25% who continue, 37.8% go to signup, 27% to demo. 512 return to pricing and end there.
  • Hypothesis: show 3–5 real differences per plan, shorten the feature list, add a plan recommender. Evidence: cutting features from 47 to ~9 raised conversion from 3.4% to 23.4% (DNSK audit).

Source: JustCall GA4, ICP markets, 1 May – 31 Oct 2026. Rates per session.

1,505 ICP visitors entered an email and stopped. No follow-up today.

What the data shows
  • 40% of people who enter an email never complete signup. They get no recovery email today.
  • 57% of drop-offs came from Hub pages. Homepage 32%, Brand page 26%.
  • Consent is asked twice: a required checkbox bundling Terms and marketing, then a second step requiring phone and AI-call consent.
  • October spike: signup visitors jumped to 7,799 but trials fell to 1,197. Signup-to-trial dropped from 28–31% to 15%.

Source: JustCall HubSpot and Cloud SQL, ICP markets, 1 May – 31 Oct 2026.

61.5% of demo visitors never attempt the form. Mobile is the widest gap.

Form-level diagnosis
  • 19,168 demo-page visitors → 7,389 attempts → 6,101 assigned. 61.5% never start the form.
  • Six required fields. The email and company step completes at 56%, the weakest of four steps on mobile.
  • Mobile is 55% of demo-page visitors but only 26% of demos assigned. Desktop conversion after the October test rose from 37.1% to 42.9%, in line with last year's seasonal rise, so it is logged as a learning, not a win.
  • Next test: reorder the mobile form steps. Move the weakest step later, reduce required fields, improve autofill.

Source: JustCall GA4, Metabase and Microsoft Clarity, ICP markets, 1 May – 31 Oct 2026.

Chapter 09 / What's next

The next gains need changes to positioning, offer and packaging, not copy or colour.

Homepage

Positioning from real calls

Prototype a new hero from how buyers describe their problem in sales and support call transcripts, then test it against the current page.

Pricing

Who each plan is for

Show the three to five real differences between plans and cut the visible feature list, so visitors can pick without comparing tabs.

Demo flow

Fix the weakest step

About six in ten demo-page visitors leave without submitting. On mobile, the email and company step completes at 56%, so the step order is tested next.

Small copy and button tests moved these pages by a fraction of a point. The plan is fewer, larger tests on the two pages that carry most visits.

Source: Website Optimisation Q4 2026 study, JustCall.
